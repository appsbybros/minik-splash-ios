> ## Information used by Minik
>
> Minik stores a child's local profile name and learning progress on the device. The child's local or real profile name is not uploaded for the online leaderboard.
>
> Minik Plus and Minik Plus English include an optional online leaderboard stored using Firebase. Nothing is sent to the leaderboard before a child selects a public nickname generated by Minik from a closed list. When the online leaderboard is used, Minik processes only the generated public nickname, an optional curated avatar identifier, score, best streak or correct-answer record, an opaque participant identifier, a Firebase anonymous authentication identifier, the product or app identifier, and a timestamp. The leaderboard does not require an email address, phone number, date of birth, photograph, real name, or free-text profile.
>
> A parent can turn the online leaderboard off in Parent Area. Turning it off stops future leaderboard uploads. Parent Area also provides an action to delete that participant's public leaderboard records. Turning off the leaderboard or deleting its public records does not delete learning progress stored locally on the device.
>
> Minik uses Firebase as a service provider for anonymous authentication and public leaderboard storage. Firebase privately associates the opaque participant identifier with the anonymous authentication identifier so that one user cannot change another participant's records. Minik does not display that private association or the Firebase authentication identifier publicly. The public leaderboard has no chat, follow, contact, messaging, or free-text profile feature.
>
> Minik may offer a non-consumable Remove Ads purchase through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the purchase; Minik uses StoreKit transaction and entitlement status to unlock the feature and restore it on the user's Apple account.
>
> Advertising is disabled in the app's default configuration. If the owner enables Google Mobile Ads in a released version, Minik requests child-directed, under-age-of-consent, maximum-G-rated, non-personalized ads. The privacy policy and App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that configuration is released to describe the technical data processed by Google Mobile Ads.
>
> Learning reminders are local notifications. They are off by default and can be enabled in Parent Area. Minik does not use Firebase Cloud Messaging or remote push notifications for learning reminders.

Do not add a retention period or deletion-completion deadline unless the owner has verified the operational process and legal reviewer has approved that promise. Before publication, the owner/legal reviewer must supply the policy controller/contact identity, effective date, jurisdiction-specific child/privacy basis, contact method for privacy requests, and any legally required retention or cross-border-processing language.

## Modern Ping Pong draft addendum — 2026-09-28

This addendum describes implemented source behavior for owner/legal review; it has **not** been published.

> Standalone Minik Ping Pong uses Firebase anonymous authentication and Realtime Database for private games and tournaments. It stores a generated nickname, a curated avatar or house-player character, a technical anonymous identifier, game codes, participant lists, connection and Ready state, schedules, scores, results and gameplay state needed to synchronize or resume a match. Other participants can see the game identity and results. No real name, email, birthday, uploaded photo or free-text profile is required. There is no chat or public leaderboard in this version.
>
> The app keeps local settings and recent-game notifications on the device. Connected clients can clean up unoccupied games and tournaments after fourteen days without activity; this is client-triggered cleanup, not a promise of deletion at an exact deadline. Profile and nickname reservations are separate from room records.
>
> Remove Ads purchases, restoration and offer-code redemption are processed through Apple. Development builds use Google test ads; production advertising remains disabled until its release configuration and disclosures are approved.

Simple embedded Modern mode does not connect to the Ping Pong multiplayer service. The host app's own functionality and privacy obligations still apply. Final controller contact, rights/deletion process, legal basis and SDK disclosures require owner/legal approval before release.
