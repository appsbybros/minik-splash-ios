# Minik Math Production Matrix

Status: canonical architecture/implementation companion to `MINIK_MASTER_PLAN.md`. The user-confirmed curriculum and activity contracts are authoritative; exact content pools and tuning remain implementation details unless separately called out.

Core milestone: `MATH_EDUCATIONAL_CORE_FUNCTIONAL_WINDOWS_STATIC_COMPLETE` (2026-09-02). See `math-educational-core-audit.md` for its evidence and explicit non-release limitations.

This document separates the ten Math levels (WHAT is learned) from the thirteen production activities (HOW it is practiced). Android has no Math product and is not curriculum authority. A generic engine or an existing prototype never makes a matrix cell release-complete.

## Remaining tunable implementation work

The ten-level curriculum, all thirteen product identities, M1 interactions, automatic-level starting policy, and global Tower/Build/Mixed/Cards/Soccer/Memory contracts are user-confirmed. Exact higher-level question pools, representation breakpoints, distractor mixtures, cadence, pacing, and visual tuning must be derived and reviewed as each level is implemented; they do not reopen the product identities or curriculum progression.

## Status vocabulary

- `USER_CONFIRMED`: an explicit product contract exists; implementation may still be incomplete.
- `FUNCTIONAL_WINDOWS_STATIC`: implemented and statically validated on Windows, but not release-complete until required art and macOS/Xcode/Simulator validation pass.

`TBD` in a higher-level cell denotes content-authoring/tuning work within the confirmed contract, not permission to remove or substitute that activity.

## Matrix interpretation guard

- `USER_CONFIRMED` records product authority only; it does not claim that a higher-level provider, view, or test exists.
- `FUNCTIONAL_WINDOWS_STATIC` currently applies to M1 through M10 and does not mean release-complete.
- Ping Pong repeats in every level row solely because it is one canonical Math product identity. It never receives a curriculum level, skill, prompt generator, or Math provider.
- The initial approved 100-object/three-zero/six-grouping-support Math pack is integrated. Dot quantity rendering remains missing-resource/debug fallback; asset compilation and device visual QA are still required.
- Exact pool expansion, representation transitions, cadence, and distractor mixtures remain tuning work within the confirmed curriculum and activity contracts.
