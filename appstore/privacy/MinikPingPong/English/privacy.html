Minik Ping Pong is a ping pong game published by Apps By Bros. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app’s current data handling.

On your device

The app stores your generated nickname, avatar and house-player character, controls and difficulty, points to win, the guide setting, your open games and tournaments, which results you have already seen, ad-frequency counters and recent play time, and whether Remove Ads is active. No real name, email address or birth date is required, and there is no free-text profile.

Online play and sharing

Online games and tournaments use Google Firebase. When the app starts and can connect, Firebase anonymous authentication gives it a random user ID without any email address or password, and the app registers a nickname that it generates from a fixed list of words, together with your chosen avatar and house-player character. A nickname reservation keeps each nickname unique.

Realtime Database stores room codes, the host and room settings, participants with their nicknames, avatars and characters (including house players), connection and Ready status, match schedules, live match actions and checkpoints needed to synchronize or resume a match, scores, results, standings and activity times. Other players in a room see the identities and game information needed to take part. Anyone who has a code can join a room that is still open; a code is not a guarantee of secrecy. The device running a match checks its state and result. There is no chat, no typed names, no photo upload and no public leaderboard.

Single games and practice against a house player are played on the device.

Ads and children

Minik Ping Pong shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob only after a completed match, never while players are getting ready or during a rally, starting only after a few completed matches and at least two minutes apart. Every ad request is marked child-directed and under the age of consent, asks for non-personalized ads and limits ads to a G rating. These settings limit targeting; they are not a legal certification.

The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the app, including Google’s ad code in it, has no access to the device’s advertising identifier. This does not make advertising data-free: Google may still process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and device information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The app stores ad-frequency counters and recent play time on the device. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads.

Purchases and codes

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. The Parents / adults button opens a grown-up check before you can buy Remove Ads, restore purchases or redeem an App Store offer code. Apple processes payments and offer codes; we never receive your payment card details. The app checks purchases with Apple on the device and remembers on the device that ads are removed. We have no purchase or code server of our own. Removing ads does not remove online-game data.

Permissions and sound

No camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location permission is requested. Avatars are built-in emoji and artwork, not uploaded photos. The app plays game sounds but does not read text aloud and does not record your voice. It sends no notifications.

Children and parents

Children may play Minik Ping Pong. Nicknames are generated by the app, so players never type their name. A grown-up check (solving an addition problem) protects purchases, restoring purchases and offer codes. Adults should supervise children’s online play, and room codes should be shared only with people you trust. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data at any time.

Providers and security

Google provides Firebase (Anonymous Authentication and Realtime Database) for online games and Google AdMob for ads; Apple provides the App Store and purchases. Network requests expose technical information such as IP addresses to these providers. Firebase connections use encrypted transport; this is not end-to-end encryption against the device running a match. Providers may process data outside your country. The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or remote push notifications. We do not sell player profiles.

Retention and deletion

Settings, saves and other local data stay on the device until they are replaced or reset, or until you delete the app; deleting the app removes its local data. Device or iCloud backups may keep a copy according to your settings. When an app connects to online play, it also cleans up rooms that have had no activity for at least 14 days and nobody connected, together with their live match data; this is not a guaranteed server deadline. Profiles and nickname reservations are separate and are not removed by this cleanup. Finishing a friendly game removes it and its live records; deleting a tournament, where allowed, removes it; leaving a tournament updates it but does not delete it. Deleting the app does not erase online data, and afterwards the app may no longer be able to find it. There is no in-app account-deletion button; contact us with your nickname and room codes before deleting the app to request deletion. Security and support records may remain where needed for legitimate operational or legal reasons; relevant exceptions will be explained.

Your choices and rights

Use the generated nickname, never a real name, and share room codes only with trusted people. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of relevant data, and complain to your local privacy regulator. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it by contacting us. Optional services may be affected.

Contact and policy changes

We receive your email address and the information you send and use them to answer and handle your request. We may update this policy when the app or our practices change; the date above identifies this version.
