Minik Splash is published by Apps By Bros. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app’s current data handling. Optional services operate only when available and enabled in your installed version.

On your device

We store avatar, arena, controls, subjects, tutorial completion, saved battles/cups and progress per learning skill locally. Records can include level, streak, independent/assisted answers, errors, review and recent answers/timing. Timing includes gameplay and is not a clinical or educational assessment. Questions use built-in generators/content; your answers are not sent to a generative-AI service. AI-assisted content can contain mistakes.

Online play and sharing

Where online rooms are enabled, Firebase assigns an anonymous ID and synchronizes membership, avatar/name, language, settings, presence/readiness, movement, throws, hits, paint, scores and results. Your learning profile, personal question/options, selected answer, feedback and recent answer records also go to Firebase so the authority device can run and validate play. Ordinary opponent views do not receive your private options, but the authority device can read personal learning state and checkpoints. “Private questions” does not mean data stays only on your phone. On iPhone and iPad, online play needs this app’s Firebase registration. The version reviewed for this policy does not include it yet, so online rooms are not active and nothing is sent to Firebase; offline play works without it. There is no open-text chat or photo upload.

Ads and children

This version shows no ads and offers no purchases. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so it has no access to your device’s advertising identifier.

If a future version enables ads, Google AdMob would show full-screen ads at breaks between games, not during active play. We would request non-personalized, G-rated ads, and every ad request would be marked child-directed and under the age of consent. These settings limit targeting; they are not a legal certification. The app would still not ask for tracking permission. This would not make advertising data-free: Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud; see Google’s AdMob data disclosure and Child-directed ad settings pages.

Adults should supervise purchases and private-group play.

Purchases and codes

This version offers no purchases. If a future version offers a remove-ads purchase or an offer code, Apple would handle it through the App Store: Apple processes the payment, we never receive your payment card details, and the app checks your purchases with Apple on the device. There is no separate code-validation server.

Permissions and speech

No camera, microphone, contacts or location permission is requested. Avatars are supplied artwork, not uploaded photos. When you tap the ♫ button, the question is read aloud using Apple’s built-in speech voices on your device. We do not record your voice. The app uses the internet only for online play, when it is active.

Providers and security

Google provides Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) for online rooms; Apple provides the App Store. Network requests expose technical information such as IP addresses to these providers. Firebase connections use encrypted transport; this is not end-to-end encryption against the game’s authority device. Providers may process data outside your country. This version does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. We do not sell player profiles or learning records. If a future version enables ads, Google AdMob would also receive the data described above.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and saves stay on your device until they are replaced or reset, or until you delete the app; deleting the app removes its local data. Device or iCloud backups may keep a copy according to your settings. Deleting the app does not erase cloud data, and afterwards the app may no longer be able to find it; contact us before deleting the app if you want cloud deletion. There is no in-app account-deletion button. The reviewed online implementation has no automatic room-expiry schedule; leaving or finishing is not a promise to erase room records. Ask support to delete relevant records. Security and support records may remain where needed for legitimate operational/legal reasons; relevant exceptions will be explained.

Your choices and rights

Use a nickname instead of a real name; share room codes only with trusted people. Do not enter contact details or sensitive information into game text. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of relevant data, and complain to your local privacy regulator. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it using available privacy options or by contacting us. Optional services may be affected.

Contact and policy changes

Apps By Bros — appsbybros.dev@gmail.com. To request help or deletion, identify the app and relevant nickname/room code; never send passwords or full payment-card details. We receive your email address and the information you send and use them to answer and handle your request. We may update this policy when the app or our practices change; the date above identifies this version.
