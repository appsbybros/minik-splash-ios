Multi Ping Pong is published by Apps By Bros. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app’s current data handling. Optional services operate only when available and enabled in your installed version.

On your device

We store your generated nickname, avatar, controls, tutorial state, local games, room references and which game results you have already seen locally. No real name, email address or exact birth date is required.

Online play and sharing

When configured online features are used, Firebase anonymous authentication supplies a user ID. Realtime Database stores nickname reservations/profiles, codes, participants/avatars, readiness/connections, schedules, match actions/snapshots, results, standings and activity times. Other players see identities and room/game information needed to participate. A code recipient may join an open room; an invite code is not a guarantee of secrecy. The authority device validates match state/results. There is no open chat or avatar-photo upload. On iPhone and iPad, online play needs this app’s Firebase registration. The version reviewed for this policy does not include it yet, so online rooms are not active and nothing is sent to Firebase; offline play works without it.

Ads and children

This version shows no ads and offers no purchases. It does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so it has no access to your device’s advertising identifier.

If a future version enables ads, Google AdMob would show full-screen ads at breaks between games, not during active play. We would request non-personalized, G-rated ads, and every ad request would be marked child-directed and under the age of consent. These settings limit targeting; they are not a legal certification. The app would still not ask for tracking permission. This would not make advertising data-free: Google may process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app/ad interactions, diagnostics and device information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud; see Google’s AdMob data disclosure and Child-directed ad settings pages.

The app keeps a record of recent play time on your device, which a future version would use to space out ads; it is not sent to us. Adults should supervise children’s purchases and online play.

Purchases and codes

This version offers no purchases. If a future version offers a remove-ads purchase or an offer code, Apple would handle it through the App Store: Apple processes the payment, we never receive your payment card details, and the app checks your purchases with Apple on the device. There is no separate code-validation server.

Even now, when the app starts it may ask the App Store whether a remove-ads product or an earlier purchase exists for your Apple Account; Apple handles this check, nothing is bought, and we receive no data from it.

Permissions and speech

No camera, microphone, contacts or location permission is requested. Avatars are supplied artwork, not uploaded photos. The app does not read text aloud, and we do not record your voice. The app uses the internet only for online play, when it is active, and for the App Store check described above.

Providers and security

Google provides Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) for online rooms; Apple provides the App Store. Network requests expose technical information such as IP addresses to these providers. Firebase connections use encrypted transport; this is not end-to-end encryption against the game’s authority device. Providers may process data outside your country. This version does not integrate Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics. We do not sell player profiles or learning records. If a future version enables ads, Google AdMob would also receive the data described above.

Retention and deletion

Local settings and saves stay on your device until they are replaced or reset, or until you delete the app; deleting the app removes its local data. Device or iCloud backups may keep a copy according to your settings. Rooms inactive for at least 14 days become eligible for cleanup when a client runs it, not at a guaranteed server deadline. That does not erase every profile or nickname reservation. Finishing a friendly game or deleting an eligible tournament removes corresponding session/live records; leaving is different. Deleting the app does not erase online-room data, and afterwards the app may no longer be able to find it. No in-app account-deletion button is provided; contact support with the app, nickname and room code before deleting the app to request cloud deletion. Security and support records may remain for legitimate operational or legal reasons; exceptions will be explained.

Your choices and rights

Use a nickname instead of a real name; share room codes only with trusted people. Do not enter contact details or sensitive information into game text. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of relevant data, and complain to your local privacy regulator. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it using available privacy options or by contacting us. Optional services may be affected.

Contact and policy changes

Apps By Bros — appsbybros.dev@gmail.com. To request help or deletion, identify the app and relevant nickname/room code; never send passwords or full payment-card details. We receive your email address and the information you send and use them to answer and handle your request. We may update this policy when the app or our practices change; the date above identifies this version.
