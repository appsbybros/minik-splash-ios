Minik English is an English-learning app for children, published by Apps By Bros. This policy describes the iPhone and iPad app’s current data handling.

On your device

Minik English keeps your child’s learning data on the device: the app language, levels, practice statistics (such as sessions, correct and incorrect answers, skips and practice time), points, streaks and records, and Parent Area settings such as encouraging messages and reminders. It also stores the leaderboard settings described below (participation, the chosen public alias and a best score waiting to be published), ad-frequency counters and whether Remove Ads is active. The app does not ask for your child’s name, email address, phone number, birth date, photo or location, and has no free-text profile.

Online leaderboard

Minik English has an optional public Top 20 leaderboard stored with Google Firebase. Nothing about your child is sent to it before a public alias is chosen. When a score qualifies, your child can pick one of four aliases that the app generates from a fixed list of words and numbers (for example, “Brave Otter 12”), some with a built-in symbol as an avatar, or choose “Not now”. Once an alias is chosen, qualifying scores are published automatically while the Online leaderboard setting is on. It is on by default, and a parent can turn it off at any time.

A published record contains only the alias and avatar, the score and the best streak of correct answers, a random participant ID created by the app, the app identifier and the time the record was saved. Players of Minik apps who open the Top 20 lists can see the alias, avatar and values. Minik English has its own Top 20 score list; its best-streak list is shared with the Minik app.

To read or write the leaderboard, the app signs in to Firebase anonymously, without an email address or password. When it publishes or deletes records, the app also stores a private record in Firebase that links the participant ID to that anonymous Firebase ID; it is intended to let only that identity change the records, and it is never shown publicly. Firebase App Check, using Apple’s App Attest, helps confirm that requests come from the genuine app. Before an alias is chosen, the app may sign in and read the Top 20 lists, for example after an activity to check whether a score would qualify, or when the Top 20 screen is opened, but it writes nothing about your child. There is no chat, messaging, following or free-text profile.

In Parent Area, a parent can replace the alias with another generated one and, after a grown-up check, turn the Online leaderboard setting off, which stops all future uploads. While it is off, a grown-up can use Delete public leaderboard records to remove this participant’s score and streak records. Turning the leaderboard off or deleting public records does not delete learning progress on the device. The app cannot remove the private link between the participant ID and the anonymous Firebase ID; contact us if you want it removed.

Learning reminders

Learning reminders are off by default. If a parent turns them on in Parent Area, the app asks for notification permission at that moment and schedules one local reminder a week on the device (for example, “Time for Minik”). Reminders are not sent from a server, and no push-notification service is used. They can be turned off in Parent Area or in the device’s Settings app.

Speech and permissions

Words, instructions and encouraging messages are read aloud using Apple’s built-in speech voices on the device. The app does not use the microphone and does not record your child’s voice. No camera, microphone, contacts, photos or location permission is requested; the only permission the app may ask for is notifications, and only when a parent turns reminders on.

Ads and children

Minik English shows full-screen ads from Google AdMob at breaks after a completed exercise, round or game, never in the middle of one, and no more than once every few minutes. Every ad request is marked child-directed and under the age of consent, asks for non-personalized ads and limits ads to a G rating. These settings limit targeting; they are not a legal certification.

The app does not ask for App Tracking Transparency permission, so the app, including Google’s ad code in it, has no access to the device’s advertising identifier. This does not make advertising data-free: Google may still process IP addresses and approximate location inferred from them, app and ad interactions, diagnostics and device information to deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud. The app stores ad-frequency counters on the device. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads.

Purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time purchase handled by Apple through the App Store. Buying or restoring it requires a grown-up check in Parent Area. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment card details. The app checks the purchase with Apple on the device and remembers on the device that ads are removed; Restore Purchases recovers it on the same Apple Account. We have no purchase server of our own. While ads are shown, a Remove Ads reminder for parents may appear, at most once every 14 days; buying from it also requires the grown-up check.

Children and parents

Minik English is made for children. A grown-up check (solving an addition problem) protects purchases, restoring purchases, links that leave the app (privacy policy, terms of use and support), turning leaderboard participation on or off and deleting leaderboard records. We recommend that parents review the leaderboard and reminder settings in Parent Area. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data at any time.

Providers and security

Google provides Firebase (Anonymous Authentication, Cloud Firestore and App Check) for the leaderboard and Google AdMob for ads; Apple provides the App Store, purchases and App Attest. Network requests expose technical information such as IP addresses to these providers. Connections use encrypted transport. Providers may process data outside your country. The app does not include Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics or remote push notifications. We do not sell your child’s data or learning records.

Retention and deletion

Learning progress, settings and other local data stay on the device until they are replaced or reset, or until you delete the app; deleting the app removes its local data. Device or iCloud backups may keep a copy according to your settings. Public leaderboard records stay in Firebase until they are deleted in Parent Area or at your request; a better result updates the same record. Deleting the app does not delete them, and after a reinstall the app can no longer delete the old records, so delete them in Parent Area first or contact us. Security and support records may remain where needed for legitimate operational or legal reasons; relevant exceptions will be explained.

Your choices and rights

You can keep the Online leaderboard setting off, leave reminders off and buy Remove Ads to stop ads. A parent or guardian may contact us about a child’s data. Depending on your location, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or a copy of relevant data, and complain to your local privacy regulator. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw it using the settings in Parent Area or by contacting us. Optional services may be affected.
